Why Crimson Academy

A rural school that competes with the best in the country — and knows every child by name.

Crimson Academy was founded in 2011 to serve children in Kagina who had no school to go to. Fourteen years later it places at or near the top of the Southern Province, and it still admits the children of the village that built it.

Results that travel. Our Primary 6 class averaged 90.4% on the National Examination, with every graduate placing in Division A — the top band. That result is why our best graduates are offered places at Rwanda's leading secondary boarding schools.

Three languages from year one. Children learn in English, French and Kinyarwanda from their first year, which is what makes the rest of the country — and beyond it — reachable.

Nobody is turned away for a meal. Breakfast and lunch are served on campus, buses collect children who live too far to walk, and sponsorship covers families who cannot meet the fees.

Character taught out loud. Truth, Discipline and Service are named, taught and referred to by teachers every day — not left implicit. Families of all backgrounds are welcome here.

How to Enroll

Five steps to a place at Crimson.

The whole process usually takes a couple of weeks. You will deal with the same small group of people from start to finish — the school's own leaders, not an admissions office.

1

Get in touch

Email us with your child's name, age, and the class you are applying for. We will tell you what places are available for the coming academic year.

You'll hear from Marie Claire Mukabirinda, Head Mistress
2

Visit the school

Come and see the campus, meet the teachers, and sit in on a class in session. We encourage every family to visit before enrolling — it is the fastest way to know whether this is the right school for your child.

Nursery families are shown round by Martha Niyotwagira
3

Sit the entrance test

Every applicant takes an entrance test, and a place is offered on passing it. The test is set at the level of the class your child is applying to enter, so it measures readiness for that year — not general aptitude. If your child is coming from another school, bring their most recent report card as well.

Lower school testing sits with Damascene
4

Confirm the place and settle fees

Once your child has passed, we confirm the place in writing and set out the term's fees, including whether you want breakfast and lunch on the meal plan. If fees are a barrier, say so at this point — sponsorship exists precisely for that conversation.

Fees and sponsorship: Jean Claude Twizeyimana, Accountant
5

Start the term

The academic year begins in September and runs in three terms through July, following the MINEDUC calendar. Arrive on the first day with the supply list below and a uniform, and your child starts with everyone else.

What You'll Need

What to bring, and what to expect.

To apply

  • A passed entrance test. Set at the level of the class being applied for.
  • A recent report card — required for any child transferring from another school.
  • Your child's records and identification, so we can complete district enrollment requirements.

On the first day

Students are asked to come to school prepared with basic school supplies:

  • Notebooks
  • Pencils and erasers
  • Paper
  • Other basic classroom materials
Copy to confirm

The supply list is written from “notebooks, pencils, erasers, paper, etc.” A definitive list — including uniform requirements, which this page does not yet mention — would save families a phone call.
