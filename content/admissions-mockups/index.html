The two it came from

A

The Path

Journey · numbered spine · one step at a time

Organised as a sequence: five numbered steps down a crimson spine, from first email to first day. Each step names the leader you'll actually deal with at that stage, so the admissions team is introduced through the process rather than as a separate roster. Why Crimson opens the page as a full crimson band — the argument before the logistics.

  • Best for a family who has never applied and wants to be walked through it
  • Puts a human face on every stage
  • Fees sit in the flow, at the point they become relevant
B

The Prospectus

Reference · question-led · answers up front

Organised around the questions parents actually ask, in the order they ask them: what does it cost, will my child get in, what do we bring, who do I talk to. The fee table sits beside a worked example totalling tuition plus both meals, so nobody has to do the arithmetic. The five steps are compressed to a strip near the bottom.

  • Best for a family comparing schools and hunting for specifics
  • Cost is answered immediately, not buried mid-page
  • Why Crimson is an editorial spread with a pull quote, not a colour band

Where this landed: C. A and B are kept above as the sources, not as live options. The merge keeps B's order — cost answered first, because that is what families come here for — and takes A's numbered step spine at full size rather than compressing it to a strip, since naming the leader at each stage only works if there is room to show them. The team roster is A's circular layout.

One thing I did not invent: the current Admissions FAQ still says “34 staff” and “23 qualified teachers”. The staff directory was corrected to 32 earlier in this work, so both mockups use 32 and the live FAQ needs the same fix.
