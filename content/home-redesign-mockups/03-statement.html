Academics

What learning looks like here.

Aligned to Rwanda's National Competence-Based Curriculum (MINEDUC), from the first day of nursery to the National Exam.

  • Nursery

    Be joyful and curiousEarly years

    A joyful foundation where our youngest learners build language, curiosity, and character — celebrated each year with a school-wide graduation.

  • Lower Primary

    Be grounded and fluentP1 – P3

    Core literacy and numeracy taught across three languages, with creative arts and sports woven through every week.

  • Upper Primary

    Be ready and provenP4 – P6

    Rigorous preparation for the National Exam — our P6 class averaged 90.4% and every graduate earned a place in a top boarding secondary school.
