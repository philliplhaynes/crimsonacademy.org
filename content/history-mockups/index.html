Design Exploration

Two redesign directions for the History page

The live page (/about/history) uses full-bleed alternating colour bands, one chapter per band, two-column text + photo. These two mockups explore different layouts for the same eight chapters, key dates, and head teachers — real content, real photos, same brand tokens (Fraunces + Inter, crimson/gold/cream).

Neither of these is meant to replace the live page outright — think of them as two different bets on how people actually want to consume this content: read it start to finish, or scan for a specific year.
A

The Rail

Compact · scannable · linear

A vertical connecting line with small nodes per year. Thumbnail photos instead of full-bleed bands, one tight sentence per chapter instead of full paragraphs, functional jump-to-year chips at the top. Roughly a third of the scroll length of the live page — built for someone who wants the shape of the story fast, not the full narrative.
B

The Magazine

Browsable · asymmetric · non-linear

An editorial photo grid, not a scroll of bands — 2013 and 2026 (the milestone years) get large featured cards, the rest sit as smaller tiles. Good for someone scanning for a specific year rather than reading top to bottom. A "the index" strip below covers all twelve key dates at a glance.
