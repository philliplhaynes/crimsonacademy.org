Crimson for Life — a new giving page, plus News & Events on Admissions

Two mockups. The first is a new top-level page modelled on Berkeley Carroll's Lions for Life — poster hero, crimson pill sub-nav, an intro that ends on a single line, a giving-levels table, a decorative rule with the crest glyph, and coloured “what your support makes possible” cards. The second adds a News & Events band to the Admissions page, which is how Berkeley Carroll surfaces recent posts on theirs.

Crimson for Life should replace “Support Us”, not sit beside it

Berkeley Carroll's nav has five items and “Lions for Life” is their giving item — it isn't an extra alongside a generic “Giving”. Crimson's nav already has six items, and “Support Us” already covers sponsorship, gifts, partners and the annual report. Adding a seventh item that duplicates the sixth would make the nav worse.

So the mockup shows Crimson for Life in the sixth slot, absorbing what /support does today. If you'd rather keep both, say so — but then the two pages need genuinely different jobs, and right now they don't have them.

The page is built on your real budget, not on adjectives

Berkeley Carroll's giving levels are named circles — Lion's Circle at $25,000, Sterling Circle at $1,000. That works for a Brooklyn private school. It would read as fantasy on a page about a village school in Kamonyi, so the equivalent section here names things instead of circles, every one a real line from the 2024–2025 report, converted at the report's own rate of 1,350 RWF to the dollar so you can check the arithmetic.

Three things to resolve before this page goes live

ItemDetail
The Zeffy form's own copy It currently runs Zeffy's stock placeholder text — “a brighter, more compassionate world for all”, “Every act of kindness” — and says nothing about Kagina, the school, or what a gift buys. It is the last thing a donor reads before deciding. Worth replacing with the narrative from this page. (Campaign confirmed as Donate to Change Lives, organised by Crimson Foundation Inc.)
One unverified sentence The page says “nine out of ten of our students come from the surrounding villages”. The report gives gender and ethnicity splits but no catchment breakdown, so that figure is flagged in-page and needs a real number or removal.
A budget inconsistency in your own report The report's total converts cleanly at 1,350 (68,816,810 ÷ 1,350 = 50,975.41, matching its own USD total). But its salary line shows $19,292.91 against 25,080,786 RWF, which implies about 1,300. I used 1,350 throughout for consistency and said so on the page — but the report itself should be corrected.
