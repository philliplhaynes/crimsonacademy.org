Portal

One account, everything about your child's education.

The Portal is run by the Crimson Foundation on behalf of Crimson Academy and every school it supports. It is where academic records live: attendance, grades, report cards, school announcements, and — for sponsors — a direct view of the student they support.

Because the Portal serves the whole Crimson Foundation network, it lives on the Foundation's own site rather than this one. Signing in from here takes you to crimsonfoundation.org, in a new tab, where your credentials are entered and checked. Nothing you type there is seen or stored by the Crimson Academy website.

Staff and students sign in with the ID printed on their credential card. Parents, donors, and administrators sign in with their email address. New accounts are given a temporary password, which the Portal asks you to change the first time you sign in.

Notes for whoever builds this
  • No login form lives on this site, by design. The card above is a single link-out button, not a username/password form — Crimson Academy's site should never collect or see Portal credentials. If a future request asks for an embedded or "quick" login box here, that changes the security posture and is worth a real conversation first.
  • The copy above is written fresh, not copied from crimsonfoundation.org/portal/login — it explains the same ID-card-vs-email distinction the Foundation's page states, in the Academy site's own voice. The Foundation page also names a specific default password string; that is left out here as an unnecessary thing for a second site to repeat.
  • Target of the button is the exact URL given: https://www.crimsonfoundation.org/portal/login, opened with target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer".

First Time Here

Three things to know before you sign in.

Step 1

Get your credential

Staff and students receive an ID card from the school. Parents, donors, and administrators use the email address already on file with us.

Step 2

Go to the Portal

Use the Sign In button above, or go directly to crimsonfoundation.org/portal/login. It is a separate site from crimsonacademy.org.

Step 3

Set a new password

First-time accounts start with a temporary password. The Portal will ask you to choose your own the moment you sign in.

Need Access?

Don't have Portal credentials yet, or can't sign in? Contact the school and we'll get you connected.
